Shiftbeat

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

The short version: Shiftbeat does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your schedule lives only on your device.

Who we are

Shiftbeat is a shift work calendar developed by bkdev10. Contact: burakkoksaldi@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter — jobs, shift types, rotations, day changes, notes, pay settings and app preferences — is stored locally on your device in the app's private storage. We have no servers that receive this data and no way to see it.

Your data is removed when you delete the app. Your phone may include app data in your own device backups (iCloud or Google), which are governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

Features that use other parts of your device

These only run when you use them, and only on your device:

  • Reminders: if you turn on shift reminders, Shiftbeat schedules local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent over the internet.
  • Home screen widgets: your upcoming shifts are shared with Shiftbeat's own widget through your device's private app storage.
  • Add to device calendar (Pro): with your permission, Shiftbeat creates a separate "Shiftbeat" calendar and adds your shifts to it. It does not read your other calendars or events, and you can remove it at any time.
  • Export and backup (Pro): image, .ics and backup files are created only when you ask, and go only where you choose to share or save them.

Purchases

Shiftbeat Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

To verify and restore purchases we use RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and the store's purchase receipt — no name, email or schedule data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Data deletion

Because Shiftbeat has no accounts and keeps no data on servers, there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Shiftbeat is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
